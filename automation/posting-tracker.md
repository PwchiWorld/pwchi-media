## Comment Patterns

Track comments that indicate:

- "I want one"
- "how did you make this?"
- "can I use it commercially?"
- "what tool did you use?"
- "can you make mine?"
- "which clan am I?"

## Reuse Rules

Reuse content if:

- viewers ask for their own Pwchi
- the hook gets comments
- the asset is saved or shared
- the post creates DM intent

Change content if:

- people do not understand what a Pwchi is
- CTA is unclear
- the video looks too technical too early
- the post sounds like a generic AI tutorial
