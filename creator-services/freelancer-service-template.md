# Pwchi Pipeline Freelancer Service Template

## Service Name

Custom Pwchi Character Pipeline

## Short Description

I help you turn one original character PNG into a reusable creative direction for avatars, stickers, game NPCs, merch concepts, AI companions or brand mascots.

## Best For

- creators who want a custom avatar
- streamers and YouTubers
- small brands
- Discord communities
- indie game teams
- AI companion builders
- people who want a personal character identity

## Deliverables

Basic package:

- one original Pwchi-style source image
- character identity block
- 3 suggested pipeline directions
- prompt notes for future outputs

Standard package:

- one original Pwchi-style source image
- character identity block
- 5 pipeline directions
- sticker or avatar direction
- game NPC or merch direction
- before / after preview

Premium package:

- custom Pwchi source image
- full identity and lore block
- 5-7 pipeline directions
- social avatar direction
- sticker or merch concept
- AI companion personality
- launch post or caption ideas

## Client Questions

Ask the client:

- What do you want the character for?
- Should it feel cute, strange, heroic, mysterious, funny or premium?
- What colors should it use?
- Is it personal, commercial or community-focused?
- Do you want avatar, sticker, merch, NPC or AI companion direction?
- Are there any styles or brands we must avoid?

## Delivery Note

This service provides creative direction and pipeline assets. Production-ready files, commercial usage terms and advanced 3D or animation deliverables should be scoped separately.
