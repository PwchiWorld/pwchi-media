## Grok Image-To-Video Prompt Template

Use this when the operator uploads or selects a source image.

```text
Animate the uploaded source image as an original Pwchi character from Pwchi World.

Preserve the character's face, colors, silhouette, costume details and signature marks. Do not redesign the character. The motion should feel like the same character becoming alive.

Create a short vertical social video, 9:16, energetic but readable.

Scene:
[Describe the scene: floating purple sky-city, neon portal, clan reveal, sticker transformation, avatar intro, etc.]

Motion:
[Describe 2-3 simple actions: character blinks, turns toward camera, small hand gesture, glow pulses, camera slowly pushes in.]

Camera:
[Close-up / medium shot / slow push-in / slight orbit / reveal from shadow.]

Mood:
[Cute, mysterious, magical, collectible, futuristic, playful.]

Audio direction:
[Soft magical pulse, tiny sparkle sounds, cinematic whoosh, light electronic ambience. No spoken dialogue unless requested.]

On-screen text idea:
"Want your own Pwchi?"

Important:
Keep it original. Avoid famous brand styles, celebrity likenesses, copyrighted characters or direct imitation.
```

## Gemini / Veo Photo-To-Video Prompt Template

Use this when the operator uploads a Pwchi image into Gemini photo-to-video.

```text
Turn this uploaded image into an 8-second vertical social video.

Keep the character design consistent with the source image: same face, colors, silhouette, outfit, expression and key details.

The character should gently come alive: blink, breathe, look toward the camera and make one small expressive gesture. Add subtle magical purple light, floating particles and a soft camera push-in.

Style: original digital character, collectible avatar, playful fantasy-sci-fi mood, clean social media composition.

Audio: soft magical ambience, light sparkle sounds, gentle futuristic pulse. No dialogue.

Do not imitate famous animation studios, existing game characters, celebrities or protected brands.
```

## Reusable Grok Prompt Angles

### 1. Want Your Own Pwchi?

```text
Animate the uploaded Pwchi image into a short vertical reveal video. The character looks toward the camera, blinks, and a purple neon portal glows behind them. Camera slowly pushes in. Add magical sparkle sounds and a soft electronic pulse. On-screen text idea: "Want your own Pwchi?" Keep the character consistent with the source image and avoid famous brand styles.
```

### 2. One PNG, Five Futures

```text
Animate the uploaded Pwchi image as if it is about to split into multiple creative futures. Keep the character still at the center while glowing icons or light trails suggest 3D, avatar, sticker, NPC and merch outputs around it. Short vertical social video, purple neon atmosphere, clean readable motion, soft futuristic audio. On-screen text idea: "One PNG. Many creative outputs."
```

### 3. Clan Reveal

```text
Animate the uploaded Pwchi character in a clan reveal scene. Purple cosmic light fills the background, the character's signature mark glows, and the camera slowly reveals the character from shadow. Add subtle particles, magical ambience and a short cinematic whoosh. On-screen text idea: "Which Pwchi clan are you?"
```

### 4. Sticker Pack Tease

```text
Animate the uploaded Pwchi character as if it is becoming a sticker pack. The character pops forward with a playful bounce, then ghosted sticker poses appear briefly around it: happy, surprised, determined, sleepy. Keep the design consistent. Vertical social video, fun motion, bright purple-blue glow, light pop sound effects.
```

### 5. Freelancer Service

```text
Animate the uploaded Pwchi image as a professional character pipeline preview. Show the source character at center, then add clean glowing labels around it: Avatar, Sticker, NPC, Merch, AI Companion. Camera slowly pushes in. Mood: polished, creator economy, futuristic. On-screen text idea: "Freelancers can turn one PNG into a service."
```
