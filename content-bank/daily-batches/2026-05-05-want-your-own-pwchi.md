## 4. Grok Image-To-Video Prompt

```text
Animate the uploaded Pwchi World image as a short vertical social video.

Preserve the characters, colors, purple cosmic atmosphere, title text feel and playful collectible mood from the source image. Do not redesign the characters.

Create a 9:16 vertical video with a slow cinematic push-in from the full group toward the center characters. Add subtle purple neon energy waves behind the group, small star sparkles and a soft glow around the characters.

Motion:
The characters gently come alive with tiny natural movement: blinking, slight breathing, small head turns and a friendly wave from one central character. Keep all motion clean and readable.

Camera:
Start wide on the full image, then slowly push in toward the group. Add a very slight parallax effect between foreground characters and the background.

Mood:
Magical, playful, collectible, inviting, fantasy-sci-fi.

Audio direction:
Soft magical ambience, tiny sparkle sounds, light futuristic pulse and a small whoosh during the zoom. No spoken dialogue.

On-screen text idea:
"Want your own Pwchi?"

End frame idea:
"One PNG. Many creative outputs."

Important:
Keep the design original. Avoid famous brand styles, celebrity likenesses, copyrighted characters or direct imitation.
```

The basic idea is:

Start with one original character PNG, then use that image as the origin for multiple creative directions:

- avatar
- sticker pack
- game NPC
- merch concept
- AI companion
- mascot direction

The problem I am trying to solve is that many AI character workflows create nice one-off images, but the character often loses continuity when it moves into another format.

So I am framing the first image as the "origin asset" rather than just a final picture.

I am also thinking about two paths:

1. A free demo pack for people who want to learn the workflow.
2. A done-for-you service for people who want a custom character but do not want to handle the whole pipeline alone.

For freelancers, this could maybe become a small service package: source image, identity block, prompt notes, avatar direction, sticker direction, NPC or mascot direction.
