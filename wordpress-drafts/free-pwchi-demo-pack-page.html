FAQ

Before you start.

Is this a finished production asset pack?

No. The free pack is a workflow and demonstration pack. Production files or advanced outputs should be scoped separately.

Is PWCHIN only an AI image generator?

No. PWCHIN includes a creator tool, clans, profiles, submissions, gallery, videos, pricing tiers and pipeline education.

Can freelancers use this?

Yes. The workflow is designed to help creators understand how a source PNG can become a structured service offer.

Is everything legally guaranteed?

No. PWCHIN focuses on original source images and creative provenance, but no page should promise zero risk or automatic legal protection.
