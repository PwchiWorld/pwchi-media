# PWCHIN Visual Social Strategy

## 1. Strategic Shift

PWCHIN should not lead social content only with indie game developers.

That audience matters commercially, but it is too narrow for visual-first platforms like TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, YouTube Shorts and DeviantArt.

The public-facing hook should become:

**I want a Pwchi too.**

Secondary hook:

**I just like watching Pwchies exist, evolve and transform.**

Commercial layer:

**If you want one, you can try the free Demo Pack or hire a creator to build your Pwchi pipeline.**

## 2. Social Positioning

Public message:

**Pwchies are original digital characters you can collect, create, transform and build stories around.**

Creator message:

**Start from one Pwchi PNG and turn it into avatars, stickers, NPCs, merch, AI companions and worldbuilding assets.**

Freelancer message:

**Learn the Pwchi pipeline and offer character transformation services for clients.**

## 3. Audience Layers

### Layer 1: Visual Fans

People who enjoy cute, strange, fantasy, sci-fi or collectible characters.

What they want:

- new characters
- transformations
- personality
- visual surprise
- clan identity
- "which one are you?" content
- collectible feeling

CTA:

**Want your own Pwchi?**

### Layer 2: Personal Avatar Buyers

People who want a custom character for identity, social profiles, gaming, Discord, YouTube, TikTok or personal brand.

What they want:

- a character that feels like them
- avatar identity
- sticker pack
- talking avatar
- profile image
- mascot for a small project

CTA:

**Get your own Pwchi.**

### Layer 3: Freelancers and AI Creators

People who want to learn workflows and make money with character services.

What they want:

- repeatable pipeline
- examples
- prompts
- before / after demos
- a service they can sell
- free starting material

CTA:

**Download the free Demo Pack and learn the pipeline.**

### Layer 4: Commercial Buyers

Small brands, channels, communities and creators who need a mascot or recurring character.

What they want:

- brand character
- mascot
- social content system
- merch direction
- avatar host
- community identity

CTA:

**Hire a Pwchi creator.**

## 4. Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Meet the Pwchi

Simple character reveal content.

Formats:

- "This Pwchi just appeared."
- "Name this Pwchi."
- "Which clan should this Pwchi join?"
- "Would you keep this Pwchi as an avatar?"

Goal:

Build visual attachment before selling.

### Pillar 2: I Want One Too

Content designed to trigger personal desire.

Formats:

- "POV: you get turned into a Pwchi."
- "What would your Pwchi look like?"
- "Drop one word and I will imagine your Pwchi."
- "Your mood as a Pwchi."
- "Choose your Pwchi clan."

Goal:

Turn viewers into leads for custom requests.

### Pillar 3: One PNG Transformations

Pipeline demonstration content.

Formats:

- PNG to 3D
- PNG to talking avatar
- PNG to sticker pack
- PNG to NPC
- PNG to merch
- PNG to AI companion

Goal:

Show value without overexplaining.

### Pillar 4: Free Demo Pack

Educational lead magnet content.

Formats:

- "I made a free Pwchi Demo Pack."
- "Take this PNG and try the pipeline."
- "Here is how one character becomes 5 outputs."
- "Freelancers can use this to learn a new service."

Goal:

Collect creators, hobbyists and freelancers.

### Pillar 5: Pwchi Creator Economy

Service and freelancer content.

Formats:

- "You can sell Pwchi-style pipeline services."
- "A client asks for a mascot. Here is the workflow."
- "From PNG to deliverable pack."
- "Character pipeline service idea for freelancers."

Goal:

Attract creators who can become partners, affiliates or service providers.

### Pillar 6: Clan Identity

Worldbuilding and personality content.

Formats:

- "Which Pwchi clan are you?"
- "Sky-Roks vs Wild-Ice."
- "Pick a clan, get a character trait."
- "This clan lives above the clouds."

Goal:

Make the universe memorable and repeatable.

## 6. Core Series

### Series 1: Want a Pwchi?

Hook examples:

- "Would you turn yourself into a Pwchi?"
- "This could be your next avatar."
- "If your online identity became a character..."
- "I made a tiny digital creature and now I want 100 more."

CTA:

Comment "PWCHI".

### Series 2: One PNG, Five Futures

Episode structure:

1. Show original Pwchi PNG.
2. Flash 5 output paths.
3. Reveal one selected output.
4. Ask which output to make next.

Outputs:

- 3D
- talking avatar
- sticker
- NPC
- merch
- AI companion

### Series 3: Comment to Pwchi

Format:

Use comments as creative prompts.

Examples:

- "Make one with blue hair and moon armor."
- "Turn my username into a Pwchi."
- "Make a shy fire clan Pwchi."

Important:

Do not promise everyone a free custom asset. Use selected comments.

### Series 4: Freelancer Pipeline

Hook examples:

- "Freelancers: this is a character service you can learn."
- "One PNG can become a client deliverable."
- "Mascot design does not have to start from scratch."
- "Here is a simple AI character pipeline you can sell."

CTA:

Download the free Demo Pack.

### Series 5: Clan Quiz

Formats:

- "Pick your sky color."
- "Choose one object."
- "Your Pwchi clan is..."
- "Which clan would hire you?"

Goal:

Create low-friction engagement.

## 7. Weekly Publishing Rhythm

Minimum rhythm:

- 3 TikToks / Shorts / Reels per week
- 2 Instagram or Pinterest visual posts per week
- 1 X build-in-public post per week
- 1 DeviantArt character upload per week

Stronger rhythm:

- 1 short video per day
- 3 Pinterest pins per day from existing visuals
- 2 carousel posts per week
- 1 freelancer-focused post per week

## 10. Conversion Flow

Visual content creates desire.

Comments and DMs capture intent.

The free Demo Pack proves the method.

The service offer helps people who cannot or do not want to do the workflow alone.

Freelancers become a second growth loop by learning the method and offering it to their own clients.

## 11. Safety Notes

Avoid:

- promising guaranteed copyright safety
- claiming all outputs are production-ready
- saying every commenter gets a free custom Pwchi
- presenting external tools as PWCHIN-owned
- copying famous brand styles

Use:

- original source image
- character origin
- visual continuity
- pipeline direction
- custom creative service
- learn the workflow
