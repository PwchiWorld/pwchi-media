# PWCHIN Commercial Offer

## 1. Core Offer

PWCHIN helps creators start character projects from an original source image, then move that character into multiple creative pipelines.

Short version:

**Create one original Pwchi PNG. Use it as the origin for games, animation, AI companions, merch, stickers, avatars and brand IP workflows.**

Strategic version:

**PWCHIN gives every digital character a clear origin before it enters the AI pipeline.**

In a market full of generated visuals and copied styles, PWCHIN focuses on original character origin, visual continuity and reusable creative direction.

## 2. Primary Audience

Initial target:

- indie game developers
- AI character creators
- solo creators
- small creative teams
- YouTube / TikTok creators who need recurring characters
- small brands that need an original mascot or avatar

The strongest problem:

Creators need original characters they can reuse across tools, formats and platforms without starting from a copied brand style or a generic AI image.

## 3. Positioning Statement

**Do not start your character pipeline from a copied style. Start from an original source.**

PWCHIN is for creators who want a character they can build on: one PNG that can become a 3D concept, animated avatar, game NPC, sticker pack, merch idea, AI companion or mascot.

Use these CTAs depending on context:

- **Create a Pwchi** for general users
- **Build My Character Pipeline** for serious creators
- **Request a Brand Pwchi** for businesses
- **Join Early Access** if the product is not fully self-serve yet
- **See Pipeline Examples** for skeptical or technical visitors
- **Get a Custom Pwchi Pack** for service-based sales

## 9. Messages to Avoid

Avoid:

- guaranteed copyright safe
- no copyright risk
- fully protected IP
- Disney/Pixar/Unreal-quality character
- official game-ready asset without proof
- automatic production-ready 3D model unless actually delivered

Use instead:

- original source image
- clearer creative origin
- designed for character provenance
- reduces reliance on copied styles
- can support multiple creative pipelines
- starting point for original character workflows
