# High Fields Clan

**Clan Name**: High Fields

**Representative Character**: Mira

**Overview**
High Fields Pwchies stand between seven centimeters and sixty centimeters tall. They carry themselves with a spatial confidence that makes scale feel irrelevant. Their proportions are slightly off from human standard — head fraction larger, eyes with unusual depth, movements too fluid and deliberate.

High Fields was a native ground clan that chose welcome over fear when Aura survivors arrived. They opened their borders, traded, learned, intermarried, and absorbed technology, spirituality, and culture from the Auran societies. They evolved faster than any other clan as a result.

Their settlements stretch across wide elevated grassland plains with architecture blending native stone and Auran crystal-working techniques. Their markets are the most diverse in the world. Their language has absorbed many loanwords from other clans.

**Key Characteristics**
- Chose integration and evolution over isolation
- Highly diverse culture due to intermingling
- Curious, welcoming, and adaptive
- Blend of native and Auran influences

**Culture & Society**
- Diverse markets and settlements
- Open to visitors and new ideas
- Strong tradition of learning and cultural exchange

**Mira’s Connection**
Mira is from the High Fields Clan. She represents the clan's welcoming, curious, and forward-looking spirit.

**Notes**
