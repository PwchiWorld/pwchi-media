# Tall Tree Clan

**Clan Name**: Tall Tree

**Representative Character**: (to be determined)

**Overview**
The Tall Tree Clan is the beating heart of commerce and connection in the Pwchi World. Centered around the colossal “HQ Tall Tree,” this clan represents a seamless blend of ancient natural wonder and Class 3 technology. While they are a ground-type Pwchi class, their influence reaches upward into the very canopy where nature and innovation meet.

The Tall Tree itself is an architectural marvel, a living skyscraper with winding roots that serve as thoroughfares and branches that support a bustling metropolis of cozy, light-filled dwellings. It is a place of constant movement, where the air is thick with the scent of pine and the hum of entrepreneurial energy.

**Key Characteristics**
- Ultimate bridge-builders
- Restless, ambitious, entrepreneurial spirit
- Love for trading, commerce, and magical arts
- Central location makes them a primary hub

**Culture**
- Organized under the Chief Clan Leader and council of 9
- Innovation is the local currency
- Melting pot of hybrids (blending with SkyRocks, Sea Moon, etc.)
- Deep reverence for the living HQ Tall Tree

**Native Appearance**
- Younger members: slim, agile, dynamic lifestyle, higher areas of the tree
- Elders: chibi, less agile, ground area
- Skin: Radiant, green-toned with subtle texture along forearms and shins (Pantone 354 C / Kelly Green)
- Hair: Thin, black, messy
- Eyes: Large, amber-colored with rectangular pupils (goat-like), thick dark lashes
- Face: Round with 2 small slits for nose, thin expressive lips
- Height: 45 cm (1 foot 6 inches)
- Build: Hard grippers for climbing, sturdy grounded posture
- Distinctive Features: Black horns emerging from hairline, faint bioluminescent green veins that pulse when using magic

**Technology**: Class 2 shifting to Class 3
**Main Occupation**: Trading, commerce, entrepreneurial activities
**Leader**: Chief Clan and the council of 9

**Border Clans**
- NW on land: High Fields Clan
- NE on land: Ignia Clan
- E on air: Sky Rocks Clan
- W on land and sea: Sea Moon Clan

**Notes**
- This is the third fully documented clan.
- The clan blends nature and technology harmoniously.
