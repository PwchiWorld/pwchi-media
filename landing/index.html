FAQ

Is PWCHIN only an AI image generator?

No. PWCHIN focuses on character origin and reusable creative direction. The PNG is the starting point for a wider pipeline.

Can I use a Pwchi for commercial projects?

PWCHIN is designed to help creators start from an original source image. Specific rights, usage and delivery terms should be confirmed for each package.

Do I receive production-ready 3D files?

The Pipeline Pack provides direction and concepts for 3D, animation, avatars and other outputs. Production-ready files can be scoped as a custom service.

Who is this best for?

Indie game developers, AI character creators, solo creators, worldbuilders, small brands and channels that need recurring original characters.
